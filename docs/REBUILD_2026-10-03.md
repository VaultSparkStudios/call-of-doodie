# Gameplay and Music Rebuild — October 3, 2026

This rebuild gives Classic, Operations, and Sewer Zombies different play rhythms and original musical identities. This document describes the implementation; it does not establish participant approval or production deployment.

## Three original scores

- **Porcelain Pursuit — Classic:** a swung arcade groove with melodic call and response.
- **Blackwater Dispatch — Operations:** a tighter tactical rhythm with metallic percussion and mission pressure.
- **The Drain Waltz — Sewer Zombies:** uneven, eerie phrasing with bells and creature-like groans.

The rebuilt composer develops each score through a 32-bar arrangement with changing harmony and sections. The synthesizer renders the musical events; mode, selected vibe, intensity, and boss pressure affect the score.

## Three different loops

**Classic** keeps its arcade survival loop and reduces reward interruptions to sparse, skippable four-wave checkpoints. Ordinary waves no longer chain drafts, route selections, and mutation screens.

**Operations** uses authored mission battlefields and continuous bounded patrols. Breach, hold, escort, hunt, sabotage, escape, and commander tasks determine progress. Living guards do not prevent a completed task from advancing. Nearby support actions are optional; transitions provide field supplies and compact orders without shops or perk pauses. Local mission scoring and route receipts remain inspectable.

**Sewer Zombies** is a finite extraction challenge. Before the four-minute flood deadline, gather sludge from kills, fuel three pumps for six seconds each inside uncontested rings, then hold the uncontested hatch for five seconds. Pump rewards apply directly. Perk, shop, and wave pause screens do not interrupt the outbreak.

## Five sewer creatures

- **Union of the Unflushed:** a shambling worker with a “BACK TO WORK” warning.
- **Drain Dentist:** a crawler that winds up before a committed lunge.
- **Panic Plunger:** a sprinter with a telegraphed toilet-emergency charge.
- **Septic Burp Department:** a bloater that spits a spread of hazardous burps.
- **The Last Paper Roll:** a screecher with a radial paper-scream attack.

The creatures use separate animated bodies, silhouettes, windups, and attack behavior rather than a Classic enemy palette swap.

## Interruption policy

Combat continues through ordinary progression. Operations uses compact field orders instead of Classic wave-bonus fanfare. Sewer objectives grant rewards directly. Manual pause and final result screens remain available; fewer automatic pauses do not remove player control.

Automated checks and rendered visual review can verify implementation behavior and presentation. They do not prove player enjoyment, balance, retention, physical-device behavior, or production deployment.
