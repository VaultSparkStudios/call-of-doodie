# Game Loop

Call of Doodie has three guest-first run grammars with distinct objectives, arenas, and musical scores.

## Classic

Choose a difficulty, starter loadout, and optional seed, then survive escalating arcade combat. Move, aim, shoot, dash, throw grenades, and switch weapons while building a run through play.

A sparse, skippable checkpoint appears every four waves. Ordinary waves keep moving: no recurring draft, route selection, or mutation chain interrupts the action. Boss threats retain their combat telegraphs. The player can still pause manually.

## Operations

Choose one of three authored missions and a disclosed deployment route. Mission-owned cover, insertion points, escort paths, and extraction positions replace the Classic arena layout.

Execute BREACH → HOLD → ESCORT → HUNT → SABOTAGE → ESCAPE → BOSS. Shoot a blast door, occupy a relay, protect a cart, defeat a fleeing courier, channel at a pump, reach an exit, and defeat the commander. Small patrols replenish while the task is active. Completing the field task advances the mission even when guards remain; an empty room cannot substitute for objective completion.

Nearby support interactions are optional assistance. Field transitions continue without perk drafts, shops, or wave intermissions. Mission completion produces a local receipt for objectives, optional interactions, tempo, pressure, extraction, and route evidence. Rematch, continue the local campaign, or return to the command deck.

## Sewer Zombies

Escape a finite sewer outbreak before the four-minute flood deadline. Kill creatures to collect sludge, use it to power three pumps, and hold the escape hatch.

Each pump needs six seconds inside its uncontested ring; sludge fuels the charge. After all three pumps are online, occupy the uncontested hatch for five seconds to escape. Creatures inside a ring block progress. Completed pumps restore health immediately. No perk, shop, or wave pause interrupts the outbreak.

Five animated creatures have distinct silhouettes, comic attack warnings, and movement or projectile attacks. The sewer loop rewards fuel gathering, positioning, and extraction instead of endless wave clearance.

## Progression, recovery, and trust

Career points, achievements, daily missions, weapon mastery, local ghosts, shared seeds, and bounded history reward experimentation without selling power. On defeat, inspect observed evidence, read an advisory correction, and rematch or try the correction immediately.

Standard leaderboard evidence and local Operations evidence remain separate. Campaign continuity, rivals, coaching, playtest receipts, and corrective orders are local and advisory unless explicitly labelled otherwise. A repeated-improvement label means two improved outcomes among the latest three valid receipts for one order; it does not establish mastery or causation. Replay evidence does not claim full physics resimulation parity.

Participant outcomes, physical-device behavior, provider delivery, and launch readiness require their own evidence.
