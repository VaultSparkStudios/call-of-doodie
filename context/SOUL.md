# Soul

This is Call-Of-Doodie's durable, public-safe creative constitution. Detailed
strategy and private operating context remain in the Studio OS control plane;
session history belongs in `context/CURRENT_STATE.md`, not here.

## Player Promise

Drop into a browser arena in seconds, understand the danger, improvise a
ridiculous build, and leave every defeat knowing what to try next. The game
respects the player's time: instant guest play, no pay-to-win power, no
manufactured grind, and no joke that obscures a survival-critical signal.

## Audience Pact

Call-Of-Doodie is for players who enjoy compact roguelite runs, readable arcade
combat, mastery through rematches, and irreverent internet satire. A player
should be able to recommend it as “the surprisingly deep browser shooter where
toilet humor, shared seeds, and honest run receipts somehow work together.”

## Design Pillars

1. **Readable chaos.** Pressure may become wild; threats, controls, cooldowns,
   objectives, and competitive-integrity state must remain legible.
2. **Humiliation becomes revenge.** A death lands as a joke, turns into an
   evidence-ranked lesson, and offers one immediate route back into mastery.
3. **Improvised doctrine.** Weapons, perks, routes, mutations, and constraints
   should create recognizable builds without prescribing one correct answer.
4. **Comedy earns its frame.** Military parody, plumbing absurdity, and
   internet archetypes heighten the action; decorative noise never outranks play.
5. **Proof over posture.** Shared seeds, replay evidence, local-only receipts,
   and explicit uncertainty make achievement trustworthy without pretending the
   browser is an infallible authority.

## Emotional Arc

The first thirty seconds should feel immediate, mischievous, and controllable.
A run then climbs from confidence to comic overload. Defeat should sting for a
beat, make the player laugh, reveal observed evidence separately from coaching
hypotheses, and convert frustration into “one more run—I know the fix.”

## Tone Rules

- Write with confident, concise absurdity; the game is in on the joke with the
  player, never sneering at them.
- Prefer concrete player language over internal telemetry, platform, or Studio
  terminology.
- Keep parody unmistakably fictional and preserve the public non-affiliation
  boundary.
- Expand public-facing acronyms on first use.
- Let quiet, literal accessibility and trust copy interrupt the joke when clarity
  or safety matters.

## Proof Ethos

Observed events, inferred factors, hypotheses, and unavailable evidence are
different claims and must render differently. Local deterministic intelligence
is preferred to hosted variable-cost advice. Production metrics, playtest
outcomes, physical-device behavior, email delivery, and subjective visual
approval are never inferred from source code or automation.

## Anti-Pillars

- No pay-to-win power, variable-cost free-tier feature, dark pattern, or
  engagement treadmill.
- No unreadable “juice,” joke density, or visual spectacle that compromises
  inputs, telegraphs, frame pacing, or accessibility.
- No fabricated retention, balance, causal-coaching, replay, leaderboard,
  delivery, or launch-readiness claim.
- No compulsory account wall before guest play.
- No generic military seriousness, sanitized corporate voice, or imitation of a
  third-party franchise's protected expression.

## Public Boundary

This file defines stable, reviewable creative constraints suitable for the
public repository. Private planning, unreleased strategy, credentials,
operator-only workflows, and detailed session records stay outside this tree.
