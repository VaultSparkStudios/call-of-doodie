Desktop

Use a keyboard and mouse or a supported gamepad. A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari is recommended.

Mobile

Use the left side of the arena to move and the right side to aim. Large bottom controls handle weapon switching, reload, dash, and grenade actions.

Progress storage

Progress is stored in this browser. Clearing site data, switching browsers, or changing devices can remove local progress unless a feature explicitly says otherwise.
