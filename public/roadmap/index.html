Roadmap

What shipped, what is next, and what is not live yet.

Shipped means playable in your browser today. Next is in active work. Later is planned and explicitly not live.

01

Playable now

Choose a mode, make a save, or read the public board today.

  • Classic SurvivalIn your browser

    Start a free endless arena run in the browser.

    Explore →
  • Three authored OperationsIn your browser

    Choose routes through seven-encounter missions with local progression.

    Explore →
  • Arcade modesIn your browser

    Play Extraction, Bot Royale, Boss Gauntlet, Hold the Throne and seeded rulesets with distinct objectives.

    Explore →
  • Your Sewer RecordIn your browser

    Review career and stash, download a credential-free save, and preview a restore on this device.

    Explore →
  • Previewed challenge linksIn your browser

    Inspect a shared run's seed, mode, build, score scope and expiry before independent guest play. Friendly scores are self-reported, not a live match or verified result.

    Explore →
  • Player-owned run packIn your browser

    Download a redacted, hashed JSON summary from a saved debrief for your own analysis or an AI agent; no upload or score attestation is included.

    Explore →
  • Field Lab prototypesIn your browser

    Try three authored, unranked tactics models for pressure routing, nemesis counters and paired practice; they do not change live Operations or scored runs.

    Explore →
  • Squad codesIn your browser

    Create or join a code locally and use it with supported score submissions.

    Explore →
  • Public score boardObserved online

    Read live top scores and community totals with stated coverage and trust limits.

    Explore →
  • Field Manual and BestiaryObserved online

    Find controls, rules and enemy information in public guides.
