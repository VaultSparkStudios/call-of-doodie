Privacy policy · Effective August 3, 2026

Your runs are yours. The leaderboard is public.

This policy explains what Call of Doodie stores, why it stores it, and which data leaves your browser.

Data stored on your device

Career progress, settings, missions, achievements, replay traces, run history, and other game state may be stored in browser storage. You can clear that data through your browser. Clearing it can reset local progress.

Shared leaderboard data

If you submit a score, the game may send your chosen callsign, score, wave, mode, run seed, proof metadata, and related run statistics to our Supabase-backed leaderboard. Do not use a callsign that reveals personal information. Leaderboard entries are intentionally visible to other players.

Optional Obelisk identity receipt

If you choose Porcelain Passport, Obelisk verifies your identity and this browser stores a minimal local receipt containing an Obelisk subject identifier, issuer, tier when supplied, and verification time. Call of Doodie does not currently upload or synchronize your local game progress through that receipt. You can download an integrity-checked local backup or forget the receipt from the Passport screen.

Operational telemetry

The site can use privacy-conscious analytics and error reporting when those services are configured. Events may include page actions, device category, game mode, performance signals, and error context. We do not sell personal information. We do not use gameplay telemetry for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Security, retention, and children

We use reasonable technical controls, but no internet service is risk-free. Operational and leaderboard records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for game integrity, support, security, and legal obligations. Call of Doodie is not directed to children under 13.

Questions or requests

Email hello@callofdoodie.wtf. Until delivery verification is complete, you may also use founder@vaultsparkstudios.com.
