PRIVACY POLICY

Call of Doodie · VaultSpark Studios LLC · Last updated: 2026-06-18

Overview

Call of Doodie (callofdoodie.wtf) is a free browser game made by VaultSpark Studios LLC. We are committed to being transparent about what data we collect and why.

Data We Collect

Callsign (pseudonym): You choose a display name (callsign) when you first visit. This is stored locally in your browser. If you submit a leaderboard score, your callsign is stored server-side alongside your score, wave reached, mode, and timestamp. No email address, real name, or account registration is required.

Game session data: When you submit a score, we store your score, wave, mode, difficulty, time survived, and basic run statistics on our servers (Supabase). This data is used to power the global leaderboard and daily challenge rankings.

Local storage: The game uses your browser's localStorage to save your career stats, run history, settings, and mission progress. This data never leaves your browser unless you submit a score.

Analytics (optional): If our PostHog analytics key is configured, we collect anonymized, aggregate gameplay telemetry (e.g., mode selection, perk choices, wave reached) to help improve game balance. No personally identifying information is included. You can block this via standard browser ad blockers.

Error monitoring: If our Sentry error monitoring key is configured, we collect anonymized crash reports and error logs to fix bugs. These do not include personal information.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your email address, real name, IP address (beyond server logs), payment information, or any sensitive personal data through the game itself. We do not sell any data to third parties.

Cookies

Call of Doodie does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage and sessionStorage only for game state. Third-party analytics services (PostHog, Sentry) may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

Supabase: Server-side database for leaderboard and score submissions. Supabase Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare: Hosting and CDN provider. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

PostHog (if enabled): Analytics. PostHog Privacy Policy.

Sentry (if enabled): Error monitoring. Sentry Privacy Policy.

Children

Call of Doodie is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information, please contact us.

Data Retention

Leaderboard entries (callsign + score data) are retained indefinitely to maintain competitive history. Local browser data is controlled entirely by you and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any server-side data associated with your callsign by contacting us. Since no account registration is required, data is identified by callsign only.

Changes

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
